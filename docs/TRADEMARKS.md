## Scope

The "Peach marks" in this policy include:

- The name "Peach", when used to refer to this software or similar software;
- The peach icon, including `peach-logo*.png`, `peach.ico` and `peach.icns` under `resources/`, and the same graphic in the app UI and on the website;
- The GitHub organization Peach's account name `peach-mitao` and the domain `peach.video`.

The marks are managed by the owner of the GitHub organization [Peach](https://github.com/peach-mitao) (account `peach-mitao`), who interprets this policy on behalf of the Peach project. "Peach" is not currently a registered trademark. Contributors keep the copyright in their own contributions, but do not thereby acquire rights to the name and marks.

## Relationship to the Source License

The source license remains [AGPL-3.0-or-later](../LICENSE); this policy adds no restrictions to the code and does not affect any rights anyone has already obtained under the AGPL. After the name and icon are replaced, the code can still be copied, modified, distributed, used commercially or run as a network service as usual, and the source-provision obligation follows the AGPL.

The icon files ship with the source and are used to build unmodified Peach. Their use follows this policy, and the AGPL does not apply to them merely because they sit in the repository.

## Uses That Need No Permission

- Distributing unmodified Peach source or the official release packages as they are, keeping the name and icon.
- Versions packaged only to fit a platform or software repository, with no functional changes, may keep the name and state the packager in the description.
- Referring to this project by name in articles, tutorials, reviews and comparisons, with a link to the [official repository](https://github.com/peach-mitao/peach).
- Derivative versions state their origin, for example "based on Peach" or "a fork of Peach", written no more prominently than their own name.

## When a Rename Is Required

When you distribute externally after changing features, the interface or default behavior, or offer it to others as a network service:

- Use your own name and icon, with no "Peach" in the name and no easily confused spelling;
- Do not imply release, endorsement or sponsorship by the Peach project, for example do not write "official Peach edition" or "Peach recommended";
- Point update checks, download links and issue reporting to your own repository, so users do not report the derivative's problems to Peach.

## Promotion and Commercial Use

- The Peach name, icon, official repository and website are not used for ads, promotional rebate links or paid recommendations, nor to make people think Peach recommends a product or service.
- A derivative promoted under its own name after renaming is that version's own business and is not bound by this policy; but the promotion may not borrow Peach's name and icon.

## Questions and Handling

For uses you are unsure about, open an issue in the [official repository](https://github.com/peach-mitao/peach/issues) to ask. If you find impersonation, you can also report it in an issue with a link.

The organization owner may require a project that does not conform to this policy to rename or remove the marks.
